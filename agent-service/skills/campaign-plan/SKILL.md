# Campaign plan

1. Call `get_company`, `get_metrics` and `list_posts`: what the business is, its stage, who it sells to, and which channels and angles have worked.
2. Turn the founder's goal into one measurable target (clicks, leads or customers through the campaign's tracked links), realistic for the company's current numbers. Don't promise growth; set a target to learn from.
3. Choose at most three channels, led by the company's defaults (B2B: LinkedIn and email; B2C: Instagram or TikTok and email) and its own results. Email only reaches people who opted in.
4. Keep it short: usually two weeks, two to six posts, at most one email. A paid ad only if the founder mentioned spending money; you draft it, they launch it.
5. Set a credit budget that covers drafting (about 120) and the posts and emails going out (5 each).
6. Write the rationale in two or three plain sentences: why these channels, why this target.
7. Call `propose_campaign` once. Nothing is drafted until the founder gives GO.
